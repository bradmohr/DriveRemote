1 · Wiring (EFB terminals X1:28–32)

ACS550 terminalSignalUSB-RS485 dongle
28ScreenCable shield (earth at one end only)
29B (+)A / D+ / TX+
30A (−)B / D− / TX−
31AGNDGND (if the dongle has one)
32Screen—

ABB's A/B naming is the reverse of most cheap dongles. If you get only timeouts, swap the two data wires — it does no harm. Set termination jumper/switch J2 to ON (bus end). Use twisted pair, and isolate the drive before opening the cover.

Then power-cycle the drive so the group 53 settings take effect. On the panel, 5306 EFB OK MESSAGES should count up once this page is connected; 5307 EFB CRC ERRORS rising points to baud/parity or wiring problems. Ramp times stay on 2202/2203.

Finally press LOC/REM on the keypad so the drive is in REMOTE — in LOCAL it ignores Modbus commands.
